About Aevora

A modern cafe, built around good moments

Aevora Cafe is a modern vegetarian cafe in Lucknow, created to bring together good food, quality coffee, a comfortable ambience and memorable moments. Founded by Aryan Verma, Aevora was built as a space where students, families, friends and coffee lovers can settle in, work, talk and eat well — without any compromise on taste or comfort.

Every cup and every plate here is entirely vegetarian, made for people who want a relaxed, premium cafe experience in the heart of Lucknow.
